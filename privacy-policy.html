Holexy Privacy Policy

Effective 1 October 2026 · Applies to the Holexy Android app (com.holexy.app)
In short: Holexy has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. What you save stays on your phone. We (the developer) never receive your files, your links or your PIN.

Who we are

Holexy is made by an independent developer, Jawad Almas. Contact: jawadahmad1144a@gmail.com.

What Holexy does with your data

  • Links you paste or share to Holexy are used only to fetch that video, sound or PDF. The app connects directly from your phone to the website the link points to. The link is not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Files you save are stored inside the app on your phone, in Holexy's private vault. They are never uploaded. If you delete the app, they are deleted too, unless you saved a copy to your phone or made a backup.
  • Your vault PIN never leaves your phone. It is used to encrypt and unlock the vault. Fingerprint unlock uses Android's own secure hardware; Holexy never sees your fingerprint.
  • Clipboard: when you tap Paste, or come back to Holexy after copying a link, the app reads the clipboard to find a video link. Nothing from the clipboard is stored or sent anywhere except the link you choose to download.
  • Camera (optional, off by default): if you turn on "Intruder photo", after a second wrong PIN the front camera takes one photo, which is sealed in your vault on your phone. It is never uploaded.
  • Motion sensors (optional): "Lock when face down / shaken" reads the phone's motion sensors on the device only, to lock the vault.
  • Notifications: used to show download progress while a download runs in the background.
  • Backups: a vault backup is a file you create and keep yourself, locked with a password you choose. Holexy does not upload it anywhere.
  • Crash reports: if the app crashes, a short technical report is kept on your phone. It is only shared if you choose to copy or send it.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no advertising ID.
  • No analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • We do not sell or share personal data with anyone.

Websites you download from

When Holexy fetches a link, the website receives the request from your phone like a normal web browser visit (for example your IP address). That website's own privacy policy applies to that request. Please only save content you have the right to download.

Email

If you email us for support or feedback, we receive your email address and message, and use them only to reply. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Children

Holexy is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from them.

Deleting your data

All data is on your phone. Delete files inside the app, use "Reset vault" in Vault settings, or uninstall the app to remove everything.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
